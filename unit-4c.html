03Key Terms

A different 12 of the 16 terms every time you load the page, in two rounds of 6. Run the page three times and you will have seen all 16. Drag each term onto its definition, then hit Check answers. Three tries per definition. Every miss costs a point, and after the third one that slot closes for good.

04Race and Civil Rights

The twenties were a decade of backlash, and the deck sorts that backlash into patterns. Drag each of the ten chips into the pattern it belongs to. You get two tries per chip, not three, because with only three bins a third try would hand you the answer. First try is 3 points, second is 2, and after that the chip closes at zero.

05The Harlem Renaissance

You get the face and the name here. What you have to supply is what they did. Drag each one onto the description that fits. All 7 artists off the slides are here, in two rounds, and each round's tray carries two faces from the other round so the last one is never a free guess.
